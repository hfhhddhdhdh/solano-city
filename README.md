Solano City is a complete open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: a sun-soaked city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with SCPD police who chase you, 8 story missions, combat, taxi fares, vigilante takedowns, street races, stunt jumps and 30 hidden sun coins — all playable offline in a desktop browser from static files, with a HUD, minimap, full map, menus and synthesised sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move and drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter and steal cars, M for the map, H for help.
